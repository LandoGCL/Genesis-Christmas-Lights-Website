HOW IT WORKS

From estimate to takedown

01

Free On-Site Estimate

Tell us about your home and what you'd like lit. We come out, walk the property, and price the project. No flat rate, no obligation.

02

We Install

We supply and hang every light, clip, and timer. Nothing to buy, nothing to store yourself.

03

We Maintain It

A strand goes out mid-season? We come back and fix it, free of charge, as part of the deal.

First week of January, after New Year's, our crew returns to remove and store everything until next season.

WHY GENESIS
