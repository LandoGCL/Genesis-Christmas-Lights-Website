ABOUT

A local crew that loves this season

Genesis Christmas Lights started in college out of a genuine love for lighting up houses for the holidays.

Our story

Landon McIntire started what's now Genesis Christmas Lights while still in college, driven by a simple love of making houses glow for the holidays. The business operated as Irvine Valley Holiday Lighting in 2024 before rebranding to Genesis Christmas Lights in 2026.

The mission hasn't changed: stay local, keep it family-feel, and be the best in the business at customer service.

Service area

We proudly serve all of Orange County, including Irvine, Huntington Beach, Costa Mesa, Mission Viejo, Lake Forest, Tustin, Laguna Niguel, San Clemente, and Dana Point.

Every job is fully insured from start to finish.
