ABOUT

A local crew that loves this season

Genesis Christmas Lights started in college out of a genuine love for lighting up houses for the holidays — not as a franchise rollout.

Our story

Landon McIntire started what's now Genesis Christmas Lights while still in college, driven by a simple love of making houses glow for the holidays. The business operated as Irvine Valley Holiday Lighting in 2024 before rebranding to Genesis Christmas Lights in 2026.

The mission hasn't changed: stay local, keep it family-feel, and be the best in the business at customer service — not the biggest.

Service area

We serve all of Orange County, with a primary focus on Newport Beach, Irvine, and Newport Coast. Not sure if we cover your neighborhood? Ask on the quote form.

Insurance: Covered up to $1,000,000 through Thimble on every job.
